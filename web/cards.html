How card popularity works

Decks: captured event entries containing a card. A player registering the same list at two events counts as two entries. In the entire-deck view, a card present in both boards counts as one deck; its copies are added together.

Deck share: decks containing the card divided by all captured entries from completed events, including entries with empty sideboards. Copies / using deck: total copies divided by decks containing the card in the selected board.

Rank: based on deck count in the selected view, with tied counts sharing a rank. Sorting or searching does not change those ranks. Basic lands, including snow-covered basics and Wastes, are hidden by default.

Card cloud: cards are selected by total copies, with alphabetical ordering for ties at the cutoff. Image area is proportional to total copies: a card with four times as many copies has twice the width and height. Images use representative printings from Scryfall; double-faced cards show the front face. Use “All cards” to include every matching card.

These statistics describe registered decklists, rather than cards drawn or cast in matches. Captured Moxfield lists may have been edited after the events. Missing lists are excluded from card counts and the deck-share denominator.
