How the leaderboard works

Season match points: 3 per match win, 1 per draw, 0 per loss, across completed events. This rewards both results and participation across the league. Ties are broken by match win percentage, then game win percentage; players still tied share a rank.

Win percentages: wins divided by all matches or games, including draws in the denominator. Draws earn match points but do not count as wins. Byes count as awarded wins and game scores, matching the source standings. Sort by a win percentage to compare efficiency across different attendance totals.

Trophies: exactly 3 wins, 0 losses, and 0 draws in a completed event, including awarded byes. A first-place finish with a different record does not earn a trophy.
